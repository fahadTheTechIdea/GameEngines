Purpose

EconomyEngine simulates the market layer of a game world: what goods cost and how much is in stock in each market, what production lines turn into what, how cargo moves and what it costs landed, and the money side — currencies, treasuries, taxes, wages, subsidies and loans. It has no clock: the caller supplies a step number and the runtime closes a period whenever the step crosses a multiple of periodSteps.

It owns

Prices, stock, supply and demand, production lines, trade arithmetic, routes, currencies, loans, shocks, modifiers, wear and the conservation invariant.

It does not own

Individual agents and pathfinding, market UI, quest rewards, or the strategic simulation. Combat and alchemy bridge in through their own components.

No floats, no hidden randomness

Integer per-mille arithmetic and the document's seeded noise only. Every number is bounded by the document's limits.

Markets, pairs and prices

A market trades a set of pairs (good, production, consumption, stock, cover, capacity). A price moves toward a reference price from elasticity, band, speed, inertia and decay, with a bounded step per period and optional seeded noise. A Result reports price, stock, supply, demand, met and change.

  • Quote(market, good, quantity) — price a trade without executing it; Trade — execute, returning unit price, value, tax and total.
  • PriceHistory(market, pair) — bounded history for charts.
  • Ratio(market, pair) — stock relative to cover; Reachable — best route value, route index and hops.

Production and recipes

A line runs a recipe up to its capacity, limited by the first thing that blocks it: LineLimit.Locked, Capacity, Input (missing input), Room (output full), Held (requirement missing), Margin (throttled because the margin is too low) or Worn (tool worn out).

IReadOnlyList<LinePlan> plan = runtime.PlanLines(marketIndex, hostFacts);
LineMargin margin = runtime.MarginOfLine(marketIndex, recipeIndex, hostFacts);
// InputCost, OutputValue, Ratio, Fraction, Wage, Overhead
  • RequirePairs are per-run requirements that are not consumed unless they carry Wear; a shared tool wears by units per run and blocks the line when spent.
  • UnlockRecipe(id) opens a locked recipe; IsUnlocked(recipe) answers.
  • ThrottleDef stops a line when the margin ratio is below Stop and runs fully at Full; optional per-line costs override the market's wage/overhead.

Trade, taxes and budgets

  • Policies: Sales tax, Import tariff, Export tariff, Subsidy; each has a rate or per-unit amount, tags and a schedule.
  • Budgets: Wage and Upkeep, with a schedule, amount, per-head share and indexation.
  • Settle(scheduleId) pays a schedule: taxes, wages, upkeep, subsidies and loans; SettleKind names each entry.
  • Arrears are tracked per market and penalised at the configured rate; InArrears(market) answers.

Routes and landed cost

With routing.mode = landed, a market's price can reflect the cheapest way to bring a good from where it is cheap, including route delay, capacity, cost per weight, margin and share.

Reachable reach = runtime.Reach(market, pair);   // Value, Route, Hops
IReadOnlyList<RouteLine> lines = runtime.PlanRoute(route);
long capacity = runtime.CapacityNow(route);

RouteLine reports good, rank, gain, ratio, landed price, via, surplus, wanted, shipped and outcome. Shipments are bounded by limits.maxShipments and arrive after their delay.

Currencies, loans and settlement

  • Each currency has a level, an exchange rate, money supply and growth. ReferencePrice(currency, good) converts the good's base price; SetGrowth(currencyId, ppm) changes growth.
  • Loans: Lend(id, lender, borrower, principal, ratePpm, term, kind, schedule, defaultAfter); kinds Annuity and Bullet; outcomes Missed, Closed, Defaulted.
  • Conservation() returns per-currency totals; money moves only between treasuries and the world account, so the totals equal the starting money plus what was minted.
  • Fund(market, delta) moves money into or out of a treasury (the caller owns why).

Shocks and modifiers

ApplyShock(shockId, marketId, scalePermille) starts a timed shock with effects: Supply, Demand, Price, Rate, Growth, Route. Modifiers are conditional supply/demand changes on a good with a tag; they apply while their shared condition holds over host facts. Active shocks and their remaining time are readable through ActiveShocks.

Facts

EconomyFacts.Read(runtime, marketId, prefix) offers market.* and currency.* facts — prices, stock, ratios, bands, arrears, shortages, currency levels and rates — for dialogue conditions, AI percepts and events. EconomyFacts.IsReserved guards the two prefixes.

Runtime API

var set     = EconomyLoader.Load(json);         // format 6
var runtime = EconomyRuntime.Create(set, startStep);
EconomyChanges changes = runtime.Advance(toStep, hostFacts);  // closes ≤ 64 periods
var quote   = runtime.Quote("town", "bread", 10);
var trade   = runtime.Trade("town", "bread", 10);
runtime.Settle("weekly");
runtime.ApplyShock("drought", "town", 800);
runtime.UnlockRecipe("forge-steel");
var digest  = runtime.StateDigest();

Advance is monotonic; the owed periods are reported by Owed(targetStep). EconomyChanges lists typed changes for the game layer.

Explaining a number

EconomyExplain returns an Explanation(Subject, Terms, Value, State) with a term per contribution — the same reasoning the CLI prints for price, loan, currency, route, reach and production. If a number matters to a player, explain it rather than showing a bare value.

Determinism and save

All arithmetic is whole-number per-mille; randomness is the document seed only. EconomyImage captures the complete state, EconomySave verifies the document digest (ECON_SAVE_DOCUMENT) and state digest (ECON_SAVE_STATE). Two equal runs have equal StateDigest().

Command line

EconomyEngine.Cli check <economy.json>
EconomyEngine.Cli simulate <economy.json> <scenario.json> <periods> [--save state.json] [--verbose]
EconomyEngine.Cli simulate ... --seeds <n> [--from-seed s] [--days d] [--metrics a,b] [--rows]
EconomyEngine.Cli explain <economy.json> <state.json> price|loan|currency|route|reach|production ...
EconomyEngine.Cli coverage <economy.json> <locale.json> [...]

Exit codes: 0 done, 1 refused or incomplete, 2 usage/scenario/file problem. The multi-seed sweep is the intended tool for balance work.
