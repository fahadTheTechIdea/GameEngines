Purpose

DiplomacyEngine owns relations between realms: standing, acceptance of proposals, treaty obligations, war conduct and peace, coalition formation, spies and internal stability. It is a pure function of the document, the steps and calls it is given, and its own seeded rolls. It makes no presentation decisions and executes no effects itself — it reports changes and issues effect requests.

It owns

Regard, acceptance scoring, proposals, treaties, clauses and obligations, wars and exhaustion, coalitions, agents/missions/intel, factions and stability, incident log.

It does not own

Armies and battles, market prices, rendering, or the strategic UI. Wars are scored by CombatEngine results the game reports in.

No floating point

Per-mille and permille-scaled integers throughout; every roll is counter-based from the document seed.

Regard and acceptance

RegardOf(holder, subject) is the clamped sum of terms — ideology affinity, leader regard, trust, honour, coalition membership and remembered deeds. It ranges −1000…1000.

A proposal is scored by Acceptance.Evaluate:

score = type.Bias
      + trait bias
      + Σ (raw[source] × weight[source] / 1000)      // FactorSource
      + Σ modifier values that hold
chance = 0 below rejectAt, 1000 at/above acceptAt, proportional in between

Factor sources: Regard, Trust, Fear, Honour, PowerRatio, SharedEnemy, Ideology, Ties, Weariness, Threat, Stability, Coalition, Terms, WarScore, Exhaustion. The Verdict carries every line: name, raw, weight, contribution.

Proposals, treaties and obligations

  • Propose(from, to, type, termDays, amount) → a proposal; Respond, Withdraw, and automatic expiry are reported as ProposalChange.
  • Treaty types have consent (Mutual or Imposed), clause sets, permanence, term bounds, regard change, requirements, exclusions, war interaction, succession behaviour and a cancel rule (Free, Breach, Never).
  • Clauses: NonAggression, Trade, Tribute, Defend, Vassal, Truce, Bond, Embargo. Each may have an interval, grace, maximum misses, due season, optionality and mutuality.
  • Obligations report Due, Paid, Missed, Called, Honoured, Ignored; Fulfil(treatyId, paid) answers a due obligation.
  • Breach applies the type's trust/honour loss, deed and casus belli (CancelRule.Breach).

Wars

  • DeclareWar(attacker, target, casusBelli) — a valid casus belli is required unless allowNoCb, which costs infamy and honour. A declared war applies the declared deed.
  • Allies may be called: an AI realm joins by a seeded chance from callBase, fear and standing. A refused call is an incident.
  • Score(warId, favoured, amount, source) records battle, occupation, blockade or event score; exhaustion accumulates per season (exhaustPpm). The host reports CombatEngine outcomes here.
  • JoinWar/LeaveWar manage participants; wars end through score/exhaustion rules and are reported as WarChange.
  • GrantCasusBelli adds a time-limited justification; Holds answers whether one exists.

Coalitions and balance

On a configured interval, realms compare threat against threatLine and relaxLine. A coalition forms when enough realms share a threat (minMembers), grows by joinRegard and bandwagonFear, and dissolves below the relax line. Formation, joining, leaving and dissolution are reported as CoalitionChange. A coalition grants its members a balance casus belli.

Espionage and intelligence

  • Agents have an owner and skill; states Idle, Away, Captured; Recruit, Release.
  • Missions: Intel, Sabotage, Incite, Counter; each has duration, difficulty, exposure, gain, shock, stability hit, deed and granted casus belli.
  • OddsOf(agent, target, mission) — success and exposure with a reason. Outcomes: Started, Succeeded, Failed, SucceededExposed, FailedExposed.
  • Intel levels: None, Rumour, Report, Detailed, Complete; Estimate gives low/estimate/high with error from the level; intel decays and counter-espionage decays separately. Exposed missions create incidents and may grant spied.

Internal politics

Each realm has stability, legitimacy, factions with shares and satisfaction, and a band: Stable, Unrest, Revolt. Political events (WarDeclared, WarOnUs, WarWon, WarLost, PeaceSigned, TreatySigned, TreatyBreached, TributePaid, TributeMissed, SpyExposed, Disaster, Succession) move factions by their likes. Happen(realm, political, scale) applies one. A succession resets legitimacy and can start a contested succession with a claim casus belli. A revolt refuses some calls (DIP_REVOLT).

Facts offered to other layers

Facts(prefix, realm, other) and DipFacts.Offered expose:

  • diplomacy.<realm>.stability, .legitimacy, .trust, .fear, .honour, .infamy, .might, .wars, .treaties, .band.
  • Pair facts: regard, reverse, ratio, at_war, treaties, coalition, intel, first.*, second.*, treaty.<type>, cb.<id>.

These feed dialogue conditions, AI percepts, events and UI queries.

Runtime API

var set     = DipLoader.Load(json);          // format 1
var realm   = DipRuntime.Create(set);

var changes = realm.Advance(toStep, seasonAt, hostFacts);  // at most 4000 steps
var verdict = realm.Evaluate("crown", "guild", "nap", termDays: 60, amount: 0);
var made    = realm.Propose("crown", "guild", "nap", 60, 0);
var answer  = realm.Respond(proposalId, accept: true);
var war     = realm.DeclareWar("crown", "isles", "rivalry");
var odds    = realm.OddsOf("spy-1", "isles", "sabotage");
var intel   = realm.Estimate("crown", "isles");
var regard  = realm.RegardOf("crown", "guild");

Every call returns a DipChanges stream of typed changes (proposal, treaty, obligation, war, coalition, mission, agent, intel, politics, incident, effect, grant). State is readable through Realms, Treaties, Proposals, Wars, Coalitions, Agents, Missions, Incidents.

Determinism and save

Every random value is a counter-based DeterministicRoll keyed by the document seed and a stream name; there is no generator state. Advance is monotonic and capped at 4,000 steps (DIP_STEP_ORDER). DipSave writes a complete state copy; it verifies the document digest (DIP_SAVE_DOCUMENT) and the state digest (DIP_SAVE_STATE). StateDigest() is stable across equal states.

DiplomacyEngine.Cli check <diplomacy.json>

Exit codes: 0 done, 1 refused or incomplete, 2 usage/scenario/file problem.

GameRuntime integration

DiplomacyComponent answers a generic diplomacy.requested port and publishes typed changes. It advances on calendar seasons through DiplomacyClocks.

EventDirection
diplomacy.requestedRequest in
diplomacy.applied, diplomacy.refused, diplomacy.evaluated, diplomacy.standing-answered, diplomacy.estimatedAnswer out
diplomacy.season-changed, proposal-changed, treaty-changed, obligation-changed, war-changed, coalition-changed, mission-changed, agent-changed, intel-changed, politics-changed, grant-changed, incident-logged, effect-issuedChange out

DiplomacyJournal/Transcript/Replay support deterministic replay; DiplomacyCheckpoint captures state. Declare the catalog through DiplomacyChannels.Install before Init.
