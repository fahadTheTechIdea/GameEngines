Purpose

CombatEngine resolves one engagement between two groups and returns the changed groups, per-battery reports, an optional shot log and a state hash. It is a pure function: no clock, no files, no internal state, no mutable randomness. Weapons, armor and unit numbers arrive with the request from the game's catalog; the rules document holds only the curves and factors.

It owns

Rules validation, shot accounting, hit chance, damage, splash, suppression, rout, pool accounting, movement and fuel, resupply rules, hashing.

It does not own

The item catalog, formations, pathfinding, AI target choice, animation, or strategic victory conditions.

Explained, not simulated blindly

Every earned shot lands in exactly one outcome and carries a code: hit, miss, out of range, no ammo, wasted.

Groups and batteries

Group is an immutable value with two pools: personnel (Health, MemberHealth, InitialMembers) and platforms (PlatformHealth, PlatformUnitHealth, InitialPlatforms). Casualties follow the personnel pool; firepower follows survivors; a platform acts only while it has crew.

  • Members, Platforms, OperablePlatforms, Destroyed, Standing (destroyed, routed, suppressed, active).
  • MarchSpeedMilli(footSpeed) blends mounted and walking pace by share of people (harmonic mean); a structure never moves.
  • FuelRoom shrinks with lost platforms; SpeedMilli is thousandths of a metre per second.

Battery is a weapon plus optional ammunition, rounds, carry fraction, capacity and crew per unit. WeaponSpec carries damage type, damage, penetration, range, accuracy, rate per minute and splash; AmmoSpec may override damage, penetration and splash.

Engagement resolution

var result = resolver.Fire(new EngagementRequest(
    Attacker: group, Defender: enemy, DistanceMilli: 220_000,
    Seconds: 30, Cover: "heavy", Terrain: "forest",
    Seed: 7, Attempt: 1, LoggedShots: 200));

// result.Attacker / result.Defender : changed copies
// result.Batteries : BatteryReport per battery
// result.Shots     : up to LoggedShots explained shots
// result.Codes     : engagement-level codes (routed, destroyed)
// result.Hash      : state hash
  • Shots earned = (carried + units × ratePerMinuteMilli × seconds / 60) / 1000, capped by MaxShotsPerBattery.
  • Each shot is fired against a finite health pool. A platform hit is chosen by a separate roll by share of pool health, so it never disturbs the hit roll.
  • Out of range, no ammo and a destroyed target end or divert the remaining shots with codes.
  • Round(groups, orders, seconds, seed, attempt, loggedShots) resolves simultaneous ordered fires from a group list; Exchange(first, second, …) resolves both directions and returns two results.

Hit chance and damage

chance = accuracyPermille × band% × training% × terrain%   (integer division)
if suppressed: chance = chance × suppressedAccuracy% / 100
if not area:   chance = chance × cover% / 100
chance = clamp(chance, floor, ceiling)                       // per-mille

armor   = armorRating × damageType.armorFactor% / 100
eff     = max(0, armor - penetration)
damage  = scaled × armorConstant / (armorConstant + eff)
if armorChecked and penetration < armor: damage = damage × (100 - nonPiercingCut%) / 100
damage  = max(minimumDamage, damage)

Range bands use the first band whose up_to_percent reaches the distance share of weapon range. Area weapons ignore cover when they hit. Splash may hit additional targets within spacingMilli, at splashDamagePercent, up to maxTargets. Suppression after enough hits lowers accuracy until recovery.

Movement, fuel and resupply

CallRule
Logistics.Reinforce(group, offered)Fills back to InitialMembers at full health; destroyed or routed groups take no one.
Logistics.Refuel(group, offeredMilli)Fills standing platforms' tanks only; a group that burns no fuel is refused.
Logistics.Resupply(group, batteryId, offered)Fills a battery to capacity; the caller removes only what was accepted.
MovementRule.March(...)Distance, fuel burned and motor seconds from terrain movement percent and march speed.
StandingRuleApplies casualties, suppression, recovery and rout thresholds from MoraleRules.

Determinism and hash

The only randomness is CombatRoll.Permille(seed, attempt, group, battery, shotIndex) — SHA-256 over an invariant string, no generator state. Any shot can be re-rolled to check it. StateHash.Of(groups) gives a canonical digest for journals and replay. Arithmetic is checked; overflow is refused as COMBAT_NUMERIC_RANGE rather than wrapped.
