Purpose

WorldEventsEngine is the world's own pressure: what the weather is doing, how wet the soil is, whether a hazard starts and escalates, whether a disease takes hold, and what event the director draws next. It never touches settlement stock or units — it reports changes and emits effect requests (signals, shocks, modifiers) for the game layer.

It owns

Seasons, climate regimes, weather, hydrology, hazard lifecycles, outbreaks, card draws/chains, director pacing, difficulty and storyteller profiles, effects, facts.

It does not own

Production, stock, combat damage, population agents or presentation. A game maps its effects to its own systems.

Deterministic

All per-mille integer maths, seeded rolls and an explicit step counter. A save is a complete state copy; branching reseeds a copy.

Document format 2

Format 2 adds self-exciting hazards (excite) and neighbour-tilted weather (spill). Format 1 is refused as superseded and is never migrated.

Strict JSON: unknown/missing fields, out-of-range values, duplicate ids, dangling references and impossible combinations are refused with WORLD_* codes.

Climate, weather and hydrology

  • Each region has a zone; a zone chooses a climate regime per season from declared frequencies. Temperature follows persistence and noise; neighbours tilt weather through spill, so a region is not independent of its neighbours.
  • Weather gives temperature offset, precipitation, wind and wetness, and applies its modifier effects.
  • Hydrology tracks soil moisture from rain, evaporation, melt, drainage and heavy-rain saturation; SoilPermille(region) answers. Flood hazards read this state.
  • Season changes are reported; the runtime never reads a wall clock.

Hazards and rungs

  • A hazard starts from its base chance, seasonal weight and conditions, with a cooldown and exclusions. excite adds self-excitation: recent activity raises the next chance, then decays — storms cluster and quiet periods recover.
  • A hazard climbs severity rungs: each rung has duration, onset/recovery steps, stress, an escalation chance and effects. Phases are Onset, Peak, Recovery; changes are Started, Escalated, Receding, Ended.
  • BeginHazard(region, hazard, severity) forces a start for scripting/tests; normal play uses the chance.
  • Intensity(instance) and ExcitationAt(hazard, region) answer current state; IHazardWatcher observes onsets.

Outbreaks

  • SIR or SEIR per disease with beta, incubation, infectious period, waning, fatality, seasonal weights, seeding and regional flow.
  • States per region: None, Introduced, Outbreak; changes: Introduced, Outbreak, Contained, Ended.
  • Interventions change beta, flow, vaccination, treatment or fatality, optionally automatically at a threshold; Intervene(region, intervention, on).
  • SeedOutbreak(region, disease, cases) starts one deliberately. Living(disease) reports the modelled population.

Cards and the director

  • A card has a tier (Bad, Neutral, Good), weight, optional condition, cooldown, group, duration, intensity, effects and chained follow-ups (ChainDef with delay and probability).
  • PlayCard(card, region) draws one deliberately; the director draws on its own cadence and explains why (CardReason: Drawn, Chained, Forced).
  • The director moves through Build, Peak, Relax phases from a storyteller profile and current stress, keeping minimum gaps between events and sharing attention across bad/neutral/good tiers.

Difficulty and storyteller

UseDifficulty(id) selects a difficulty profile (hazard, bad-card, disease and stress multipliers). UseStoryteller(id) selects pacing: decay, peak threshold, relax threshold, peak hold, rest minimum, gap range and tier shares. Both are explicit controls, not hidden defaults.

Effects and modifiers

Effects are Signal (notify), Shock (an immediate change), Modifier (a bounded counter delta) or nested Hazard/Outbreak starts. Modifiers have base/min/max and are read with Modifier(region, index). A game maps these to its own systems; the engine does not mutate another engine's state.

Facts

WorldFacts offers world.* facts — season, weather, temperature, wetness, soil, hazard phase/rung/intensity, disease state/cases, director phase, stress and modifier values — for dialogue conditions, AI percepts, economy shocks and events. Facts(prefix, region) returns a region's dictionary.

Runtime API

var set     = WorldLoader.Load(json);          // format 2
var world   = WorldRuntime.Create(set);
WorldChanges c = world.Advance(toStep, seasonAt, hostFacts);  // weather, hazards, disease, cards
world.BeginHazard("valley", "flood", "major");
world.SeedOutbreak("valley", "flu", cases: 3);
world.Intervene("valley", "vaccinate", on: true);
world.PlayCard("harvest-fair", "valley");
world.UseDifficulty("hard");
world.UseStoryteller("calm");
var digest  = world.StateDigest();

WorldChanges is a typed stream (season, regime, weather, hazard, outbreak, card, director, effect, intervention). Branch(reseededSet) creates an independent copy for what-if runs; BranchForcingOnset forces one onset in the branch.

Determinism, save and branch

  • Per-mille integer maths and seeded rolls; a step is a function of the document, state, step and host facts.
  • WorldImage/WorldSave carry the complete state; the document digest and state digest are checked (WORLD_SAVE_DOCUMENT, WORLD_SAVE_STATE). A loaded state continues identically.
  • Branch exists for balance and scenario work: it never mutates the original runtime.

Command line and balance tools

WorldEventsEngine.Cli check <world.json>
WorldEventsEngine.Cli simulate <world.json> <scenario.json> <steps> [--save state.json] [--verbose]
WorldEventsEngine.Cli simulate ... --seeds <n> [--from-seed s] [--days d] [--metrics a,b] [--rows] [--onsets]
WorldEventsEngine.Cli simulate ... --rare <hazard>:<rung> [--predraw | --conditional] [--effort n] [--efforts a,b,c|auto] [--replications r]
WorldEventsEngine.Cli explain <world.json> <state.json> hazard|card|disease|weather ...
WorldEventsEngine.Cli coverage <world.json> <locale.json> [...]

The multi-seed sweep studies distributions; --rare with fixed-effort splitting, severity predraw and the conditional estimator measures rare hazard onsets with an interval, using EngineCommon.Balance. Exit codes: 0 done, 1 refused or incomplete, 2 usage/scenario/file problem.

GameRuntime integration

WorldEventsComponent advances the world on the session clock and publishes weather/hazard/outbreak/card changes; WorldEvents.Bridges translates effects into economy, diplomacy, calendar and AI requests.

DirectionExamples
Requests inadvance, begin hazard, intervene, seed outbreak, play card, use difficulty/storyteller, query
Results outseason/regime/weather changed, hazard started/escalated/receding/ended, outbreak changed, card drawn/chained, director phase, effect issued, refused

WorldCheckpoint captures state; a checkpoint is consistent when no request waits.
