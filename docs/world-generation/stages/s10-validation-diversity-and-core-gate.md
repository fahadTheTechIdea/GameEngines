## Algorithm and design

Maintain a capability-by-fixture matrix and distinguish implemented, locally tested, visually reviewed, owner accepted and unsupported. Use independent invariant checks plus measured expressive range. Compare the same content/endpoint demands across flat, hillside, coastal and valley terrain, then compare supported regular, irregular and mixed layouts across non-city game programs. Separate physical building area, root-program envelope, designed grounds, traversable space, protected open space and unresolved gross land. Track mandatory counts, graph obligations, rejection rate, work budgets and failure causes. Diversity must reflect structural differences, not just seeds, colors or rotated grids. Include actual CLI/export/reload/browser journeys and focused negative cases.
