**2026-10-26 owner review:** the mixed-frontage map and underlying city/district composition were rejected. The rejected implementations and runnable fixtures have been removed; past generated outputs remain defect evidence. Counts, package verification and complete declared rectangular scope do not establish believable city design. See the [algorithm research and replacement direction](../../docs/PROCEDURAL_CITY.md#research-and-replacement-direction-2026-10-26); no corrected generator is delivered by that research.
