# Mixed variant capacity

This framework example combines a required quantity with weighted catalog
variation. The selected camps provide 7, 7 and 10 rest spaces: 24 total for
a requirement of 20. Each camp retains its own authored footprint and nested
parts. Raising the demand extends the stable variant sequence; spatial placement
may be reconsidered unless protected by the game author.

The capacity receipt groups actual member IDs by their source. The same contract
works for catalog capacities and registered structure variants. Use
`WorldMetadataGenerator.GenerateFilesAsync` or `generate-metadata`, then
`ReadPublishedAsync` or `verify-metadata` for full supplied-source replay.
Capacity-owned nodes carry allocation keys, so stored-world inspection can detect
a missing plan or inconsistent member binding. Complete source replay is needed
to detect an omitted zero-member allocation or removal of all its markers.

This is an engine-independent example, not a configured game or approved art.
The retired single-`Source` capacity receipt is not supported; regenerate older
world packages from their source inputs.
