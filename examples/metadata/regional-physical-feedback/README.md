not a finished game or a visual-design example. Godot and artwork are deferred.

only the owning region needs its alternative; other inputs/component selections

several changes, as exercised by the complete-component-assignment reference.

resized and no required member or journey is dropped. Ownership borders and the
other regions' complete inputs/component selections remain unchanged.
